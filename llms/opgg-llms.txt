# OP.GG
> OP.GG is the world's leading League of Legends statistics and performance tracking platform, providing real-time data on summoner ranks, win rates, champion mastery, and meta trends to help players improve their gameplay and understand the global meta.  
> OP.GG는 세계 최대 규모의 리그 오브 레전드 전적 및 통계 분석 플랫폼으로, 소환사 랭크·승률·챔피언 숙련도·메타 트렌드 등 실시간 데이터를 제공하여 플레이어가 실력을 향상하고 글로벌 메타를 이해할 수 있도록 돕습니다.

## AI-readable Summoner and Match Data / AI용 소환사 및 매치 데이터
- Summoner snapshot (default English): `https://op.gg/lol/summoners/{region}/{gameName}-{tagline}/ai.json`
- Summoner snapshot (localized): `https://op.gg/{locale}/lol/summoners/{region}/{gameName}-{tagline}/ai.json`
- Match snapshot (default English): `https://op.gg/lol/summoners/{region}/{gameName}-{tagline}/matches/{gameId}/{createdAtTimestamp}/ai.json`
- Match snapshot (localized): `https://op.gg/{locale}/lol/summoners/{region}/{gameName}-{tagline}/matches/{gameId}/{createdAtTimestamp}/ai.json`
- Each JSON response includes a self-contained `summary` plus first-party ranked, champion, match, build, participant, and team objective facts when available.
- Use the canonical OP.GG page URL for citations, cache responses where possible, and avoid high-rate crawling of per-summoner endpoints.
